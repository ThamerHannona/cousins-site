Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026
Publisher: Shake and Bake Labs

COUSINS ("the app") helps you keep track of your family's birthdays and gifts. We built it so your information stays on your device.

Information we collect

We do not sell any personal information. COUSINS has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs. Your family list lives on your device. The only information that ever passes through a server is an answer to an "Ask for their birthday" link, described below, and it is deleted once it reaches you.

Data stored on your device

The names, birthdays, relationships, notes, photos, gift log and preferences you enter are saved only in the app's local storage on your iPhone. They are not transmitted to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Contacts (optional): used on-device to let you import names and birthdays. Contacts are never uploaded.
  • Photos (optional): used to choose profile photos, which are stored on your device.
  • Calendar (optional): used to add birthday events to your calendar on your device.
  • Notifications (optional): birthday reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Third-party services

  • Wikipedia / Wikidata: to find a "birthday twin", the app may request Wikipedia's public "on this day" data. Only a month and day (e.g. 10/28) are sent — no names or other personal data. Wikimedia's own privacy policy applies to those requests.
  • Amazon and Target: gift links open those websites in your browser. Their privacy policies apply once you leave the app. Some links may be affiliate links.

"Ask for their birthday" links

You can send a relative or friend a private link (cousinsai.com/ask) so they can type in their own birthday. When they submit the form, their answer (the name they enter, their birthday month and day, an optional birth year, and any optional "things they love" or gift ideas) is stored briefly in Google Cloud Firestore, operated for us by Google, so it can be passed to your app. Each link contains a long random code that only you and the person you sent it to know; answers cannot be listed or searched. Your app downloads the answer the next time you open it and deletes it from the server as soon as you add or dismiss it, or when you cancel the link. Answers that are never collected are deleted automatically after 60 days. No IP addresses, device identifiers, cookies or tracking are collected by us on that page, and the answer is used only to add that person to your list. Google's privacy policy applies to its processing as our service provider.

Sharing

If you choose "Share your family list", the app creates a share message or code that you send yourself using iOS sharing or the clipboard. We never receive it.

Children

COUSINS is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
