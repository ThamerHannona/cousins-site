Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026
Publisher: Shake and Bake Labs

COUSINS ("the app") helps you keep track of your family's birthdays and gifts. We built it so your information stays on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. COUSINS has no user accounts, no servers that store your data, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your device

The names, birthdays, relationships, notes, photos, gift log and preferences you enter are saved only in the app's local storage on your iPhone. They are not transmitted to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Contacts (optional): used on-device to let you import names and birthdays. Contacts are never uploaded.
  • Photos (optional): used to choose profile photos, which are stored on your device.
  • Calendar (optional): used to add birthday events to your calendar on your device.
  • Notifications (optional): birthday reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Third-party services

  • Wikipedia / Wikidata: to find a "birthday twin", the app may request Wikipedia's public "on this day" data. Only a month and day (e.g. 10/28) are sent — no names or other personal data. Wikimedia's own privacy policy applies to those requests.
  • Amazon and Target: gift links open those websites in your browser. Their privacy policies apply once you leave the app. Some links may be affiliate links.

Sharing

If you choose "Share your family list", the app creates a share message or code that you send yourself using iOS sharing or the clipboard. We never receive it.

Children

COUSINS is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
