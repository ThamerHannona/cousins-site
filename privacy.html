Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026
Publisher: Shake and Bake Labs

COUSINS ("the app") helps you keep track of your family's birthdays and gifts. We built it so your information stays on your device.

Information we collect

We do not sell any personal information. COUSINS has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs. Your family list lives on your device. The only information that ever passes through a server is an answer to an "Ask for their birthday" link, a family tree you choose to share with "Share tree", or a gift list you choose to share with "Share list", all described below and all stored only temporarily.

Data stored on your device

The names, birthdays, relationships, notes, photos, gift log and preferences you enter are saved only in the app's local storage on your iPhone. They are not transmitted to us, except a snapshot you choose to send with "Share tree" or a gift list you choose to send with "Share list" (below). Deleting the app deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Contacts (optional): used on-device to let you import names and birthdays. Contacts are never uploaded.
  • Photos (optional): used to choose profile photos, which are stored on your device. If you use "Share tree", small copies of the selected people's profile photos are included in the shared snapshot (see below).
  • Calendar (optional): used to add birthday events to your calendar on your device.
  • Notifications (optional): birthday reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Third-party services

  • Wikipedia / Wikidata: to find a "birthday twin", the app may request Wikipedia's public "on this day" data. Only a month and day (e.g. 10/28) are sent — no names or other personal data. Wikimedia's own privacy policy applies to those requests.
  • Online stores: when you paste or share a product link into COUSINS, your iPhone loads that product page directly from the store (as Safari would) to fill in the name, photo and price; nothing about it is sent to us. Store logos are loaded from Google's public website-icon service, which receives only the store's web address. "Buy" buttons open the store's website in your browser, where the store's own privacy policy applies. Some Amazon and Target links may be affiliate links.

"Ask for their birthday" links

You can send a relative or friend a private link (cousinsai.com/ask) so they can type in their own birthday. When they submit the form, their answer (the name they enter, their birthday month and day, an optional birth year, and any optional "things they love" or gift ideas) is stored briefly in Google Cloud Firestore, operated for us by Google, so it can be passed to your app. Each link contains a long random code that only you and the person you sent it to know; answers cannot be listed or searched. Your app downloads the answer the next time you open it and deletes it from the server as soon as you add or dismiss it, or when you cancel the link. Answers that are never collected are deleted automatically after 60 days. No IP addresses, device identifiers, cookies or tracking are collected by us on that page, and the answer is used only to add that person to your list. Google's privacy policy applies to its processing as our service provider.

"Share list" gift lists

If you tap "Share list" on one of your gift lists or on a gift list you keep for someone (each occasion list, such as Christmas or Birthday, is shared separately with its own link), the app uploads that list so family can open it with one link (cousinsai.com/list), with or without the app. A shared list contains only the gift ideas on it (each item's name, store link, store name, product photo link, price, and the size, color, quantity and "most wanted" star if you add them), the list's title, and the first name you chose for yourself and for the person it's for. Private notes, gifts you marked bought or given, receipts and the rest of your family list are never uploaded. When someone taps "I'm getting this", the claim (which item, and a first name only if they choose to type one) is stored with the list so others don't buy the same thing; a random code saved on their own device lets only them undo it. In the COUSINS app, claims on your own wishlist are hidden from you by default (surprise mode). Lists are stored in Google Cloud Firestore, operated for us by Google, under a long random code that only you and the people you send the link to know; they cannot be listed or searched, and only the phone that created a list can change it. Anyone who has the link can see the list and its claims until it expires 60 days after it was last updated, so only send it to people you trust. Expired lists can no longer be opened and are purged from the server; tap "Stop updating the shared link" in the app to stop sharing changes, and email us at any time to have a shared list deleted sooner. No accounts, cookies, IP logging or tracking are used by us on the list page.

"Share tree" links

If you tap "Share tree", the app uploads a snapshot of the people you select so you can send a relative one link (cousinsai.com/join). The snapshot contains your name, birthday and profile photo plus, for each selected person, their name, birthday (and birth year if known), relationship, family side, and who they are married to or the child of, and their profile photo if they have one (resized to a small 384-pixel JPEG and stored alongside the snapshot). Notes are included only if you turn on "Include notes". Gift ideas, gift logs and receipts are never uploaded. The snapshot is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, operated for us by Google, under a long random code that only you and the people you send the link to know; it cannot be listed, searched or changed. Anyone who has the link can open it until it expires 30 days after you create it, so only send it to people you trust. Expired snapshots can no longer be opened and are purged from the server; email us at any time to have a shared snapshot deleted sooner. When someone adds your tree, the people are copied into the app on their device, which works like the rest of their family list and is not sent back to us.

Sharing by code

The older "Share as code" option creates a message or code that you send yourself using iOS sharing or the clipboard. We never receive it.

Children

COUSINS is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
